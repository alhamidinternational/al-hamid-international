Our Mission

Sincere teaching, at your own pace

Why Al Hamid International

We were founded to make authentic, one-on-one Qur'an and Deen education accessible to families anywhere in the world — without long commutes, crowded classrooms, or rigid schedules. Every class is live, personal, and paced to the student, whether they are a young child starting with Noorani Qaida or an adult refining their Tajweed.

1

Qualified Teachers

Certified Huffaz and Aalims, screened and trained for teaching children and adults online.

2

Flexible Scheduling

Classes arranged around your family's time zone and routine, not the other way around.

3

Personal Attention

Mostly one-on-one sessions, so every student's pace and level is genuinely respected.

What We Teach

Courses for every stage of learning

Noorani Qaida & Qur'an Reading

Arabic letters, correct pronunciation and fluent reading of the Qur'an, built step by step from the very first lesson.

Tajweed

The rules of correct Qur'anic recitation, taught with listening, repetition and gentle correction until it becomes natural.

Hifz (Memorization)

A structured memorization plan with daily revision tracking, for students working toward completing the Qur'an by heart.

Arabic Language

Reading, writing and conversational Arabic, opening the door to understanding the Qur'an and Sunnah directly.

Islamic Studies (Deeniyat)

Aqeedah, Fiqh, Seerah and daily-life etiquette, taught in an age-appropriate, discussion-friendly way.

Kids' Islamic Foundation

A gentle first step for young children — short lessons, stories of the Prophets, and simple duas, built for small attention spans.
